# THE OCEANS OF EARTH — Devonian Prototype

Experimental Three.js vertical slice inspired by the “Claude of Duty” prompting pattern: aim too high, decompose by subsystem, review harshly, and iterate rather than stopping when it merely works.

## Vertical slice

- First-person futuristic aquarium hall
- Era galleries that hint at Cambrian, Ordovician, Jurassic, Cretaceous and Cenozoic archives
- Monumental Devonian observation tank
- Procedural schooling fish, plankton, rocks and caustic-like lighting
- Procedural Dunkleosteus model with armored head and jaw animation
- Cinematic reveal when the player approaches the tank
- “Enter the era” transition through the glass
- Free swimming inside the Devonian sea
- Desktop pointer-lock controls and touch controls
- Procedural ambient WebAudio; no downloaded art/audio assets

This is a browser prototype, not a literal AAA asset production. Its purpose is to test whether the aggressive quality-loop prompting style produces a noticeably stronger first pass and a better iteration discipline.

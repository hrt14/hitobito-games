## Naming rule applied

Use the card title itself to answer, in this order where possible:

1. Who / what exact situation is this for?
2. What changes or becomes easier?
3. What is distinctive about this app (seconds, questions, method, game mechanic)?

The card body is reduced to one short obi line. Long descriptions remain in internal metadata/DOM only where needed for search/diagnosis compatibility.

## Important limitation

Amazon does not expose trustworthy public search-volume numbers for individual Japanese search phrases. Do not invent numeric keyword power. Use exact-title/collision searches and visible market language only as qualitative evidence.

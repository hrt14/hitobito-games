# LEVEL UP app title checklist source

Current naming work follows the user-provided checklist:

- Research usable Google suggestion language.
- Research usable Amazon wording/title patterns.
- Treat Amazon keyword strength qualitatively unless a trustworthy numeric source exists.
- Center one primary motivation/benefit.
- Include concrete gain, novelty/curiosity where natural, ease/speed where supported, clear audience, specificity, and direct wording.
- Keep the title consistent with what the app actually does.
- Avoid empty wordplay.
- Check Amazon for exact/near title collisions before finalizing distinctive titles.

The public card presentation should resemble a book cover: title + one obi line, rather than title + multiple explanatory fields.
